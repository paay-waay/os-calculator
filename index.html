Internal use: P is the expected standard fee for the selected mix of spaces. The wider planning range is the number a prospective client may see before drawings, site conditions, deliverables and revision needs are reviewed. A written proposal replaces this planning range.

How to use the pricing range internally

The calculator now separates the internal standard price from the client-facing planning range. The range is not a menu of interchangeable prices. Its position depends on the confirmed amount of design development required.

What must be true for the lower end to be defensible?
  • Reliable existing plans, measurements or site information are available.
  • Room geometry and site conditions are conventional, with limited exploratory layout work.
  • Major plumbing and electrical locations are retained or require only limited development.
  • Custom cabinetry, millwork, bespoke details and independent room concepts remain limited.
  • A coordinated material language can be carried across the selected spaces.
  • The project remains within the standard drawing, visualization and revision allowance.
What normally moves a proposal above P?
  • Multiple viable layout directions must be developed and compared.
  • Kitchen, bathroom, lighting or electrical layouts require substantial reconfiguration.
  • Custom millwork, feature walls, fireplaces or detailed built-ins require additional elevations.
  • Existing drawings are incomplete and base information must be reconstructed.
  • Different rooms require substantially independent palettes, specifications or visualizations.
  • Decision-making, stakeholder coordination or anticipated revisions are more involved than standard.

Pricing discipline: do not describe the difference between the range minimum and the final quote as an added surcharge. Explain the conditions represented by the lower end, identify the confirmed scope factors, and state why the project sits at a particular position within the original planning range. Reaching the lower end should correspond to genuine scope standardization or reduction.
